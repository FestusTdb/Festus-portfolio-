How I work

Show up, then stay.

Communities do not fail at launch. They fail in the quiet weeks after. The work is a cadence, not a campaign.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Onboard the room. Hear what members actually ask, where they stall, and which stories already work.

  2. 02

    Translate

    Turn protocol language into narratives people can repeat — threads, announcements, and campaigns with a point of view.

  3. 03

    Activate

    Run the cadence: ambassadors, partnerships, launches, and the daily presence that makes a feed feel alive.

  4. 04

    Sustain

    Moderation, trust, and follow-through. Communities stay if someone is still there after the announcement.
